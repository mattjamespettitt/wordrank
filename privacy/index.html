Privacy Policy

French Word Rank and other Word Rank apps · Effective October 2026

In short: the apps don't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you type or look up leaves your device.

What stays on your device

The app keeps your recent lookups, the last list you sorted and your chosen ranking (Everyday, Spoken or Written) on your iPhone or iPad so they're there next time. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they're deleted when you delete the app. You can clear recent lookups at any time in the app.

The word list is built into the app, so looking up words works offline and doesn't send your searches anywhere.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or address. Apple only tells the app whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple ID, and that check happens on your device. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Links to other websites

When you tap a dictionary or pronunciation link (WordReference, Larousse or Forvo), that website opens inside the app. Those sites are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. The app only sends them the word you tapped, as part of the page address.

App Store statistics

Apple gives developers anonymous, combined statistics such as the number of downloads and, if you chose to share them with developers in your iPhone's settings, crash reports and usage figures. These don't identify you.

Children

The apps are suitable for all ages and don't collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: matt.pettitt@gmail.com.
