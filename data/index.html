English meanings

The meanings in french-meanings.json come from the English Wiktionary, by Wiktionary contributors, as extracted by Kaikki (wiktextract), licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. For each word, up to three of the first senses for its two most common word classes were kept and shortened to about 70 characters; inflection-only senses ("plural of …") are used only when a word class has nothing else. Shared under the same licence.

Format: {"word": [["word class", "meaning", …], …]}.

What was changed

Lexique's entries were grouped by dictionary form (lemma), keeping the film-subtitle and book frequencies (occurrences per million words) for each word class. A table of inflected forms (verb forms, plurals, feminine forms) pointing to their dictionary forms was added so any form can be looked up.

Format

L is a list of dictionary forms: [word, subtitles per million, books per million, [[word class, subtitles, books, gender], …]]. F maps each inflected form to the positions in L of its dictionary forms.
