What does the rank mean?

Every French word gets a rank among about 47,000 dictionary forms, from the most common (de, être) to the rarest. All forms of a word count together, so suis and étaient count towards être. Everyday ranks are the average of spoken French (film and TV subtitles) and written French (books). The verdict tells you whether to learn the word now, soon, when you meet it, or not yet.

A word isn't found

Check the spelling, or try the dictionary form: the infinitive of a verb or the masculine singular of an adjective. Accents are optional. Names of people and places aren't in the list.

What's free and what's in the unlock?

Words in the top 3,000, which make up about 93% of everyday French, are free in full. The one-time unlock adds exact ranks for all 47,000 words, every band in Top words and exact ranks for every word when you sort a list. There's no subscription.

Restoring your purchase

The unlock belongs to your Apple ID, so it works on all your devices signed in with it. If the app shows as locked after reinstalling or on a new device, open the unlock screen, or About (the ⓘ button), and tap Restore purchase.
